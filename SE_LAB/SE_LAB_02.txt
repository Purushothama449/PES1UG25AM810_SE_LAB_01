# Pharmacy Expiry & Re-order Engine

## Project Overview

The **Pharmacy Expiry & Re-order Engine (PEROE)** is a software system designed to help pharmacies manage medicine inventory, track expiry dates, follow the First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO) method, monitor stock levels, and manage medicine re-orders.

This project is implemented using **Jira** to demonstrate Agile and Scrum project management practices.

---

## Project Objectives

- Manage medicine inventory efficiently.
- Track medicine batches and stock quantities.
- Monitor medicine expiry dates.
- Support First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO) dispensing.
- Monitor stock levels against safety thresholds.
- Generate purchase orders when stock is low.
- Dispatch purchase orders to suppliers.
- Track the status of re-orders.
- Demonstrate Scrum activities using Jira.

---

## Epics

### Epic 1: Medicine Inventory Management

**Description:**  
Manage medicine stock, batches, quantities, and inventory information in the pharmacy.

### Epic 2: Expiry & FEFO Management

**Description:**  
Track medicine batch expiry dates and ensure medicines are dispensed using First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO).

### Epic 3: Automated Re-order Management

**Description:**  
Monitor medicine stock levels and automatically generate purchase orders when stock falls below the safety threshold.

### Epic 4: Supplier & Dispatch Management

**Description:**  
Send purchase order requests to the inventory supplier and track replenishment orders.

## User Stories

| User Story | As a | I want to | So that |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medicine Stock Tracking | Pharmacy Clerk | View medicine stock levels | I can monitor available inventory |
| Medicine Batch Management | Pharmacy Clerk | Manage medicine batches and quantities | Inventory records remain accurate |
| Medicine Expiry Tracking | Pharmacy Clerk | Track medicine batch expiry dates | I can identify batches approaching expiry |
| FEFO Dispensing | Pharmacy Clerk | Prioritize medicines with the nearest valid expiry date | Medicines are dispensed using FEFO |
| Stock Threshold Monitoring | Pharmacy Clerk | Monitor medicine stock against the safety threshold | Low-stock medicines can be identified |
| Automatic Purchase Order Generation | Pharmacy Clerk | Automatically generate a purchase order when stock is below the threshold | Medicines can be reordered on time |
| Supplier Purchase Order Dispatch | Inventory Supplier | Receive purchase order requests | I can process medicine replenishment |
| Re-order Status Tracking | Pharmacy Clerk | Track supplier order status | I know when medicine replenishment is expected |
